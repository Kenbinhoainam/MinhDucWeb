# Minh Đức V11 — design direction

**North star:** “Nhân lực là tài sản” means human capability with operational rigor. Avoid generic pastel SaaS cards; use disciplined editorial rhythm and precise industrial imagery.

## Audience journeys
B2B supplier review: clear company purpose → verified service scope → proof signals (only once verified) → business contact.
B2C job search: job search → salary-first cards → detailed requirements → five-field form; CV for internal roles.

## Tokens
- Navy #112D59 — enterprise headings, footer.
- Blue #2155A2 — links, focus and utility components.
- Red #D71F36 — reserve for decisive actions.
- Surface #F7FAFD, white #FFFFFF, text #233E63, divider #DDE7F2.
- Fonts: Noto Serif (large editorial heading), Noto Sans (body/UI), with CJK-safe fallbacks.
- Width: 1280px max; 12-column desktop; single column mobile.
- Keyboard-visible focus, 48px priority touch target, reduced-motion safe.

## Photography and ethics
Eight AI-created images provide *demonstration art direction only*. Do not describe the depicted facilities, staff, historical results or named companies as verified facts. Replace with commissioned/approved photography for bidding.
